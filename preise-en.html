Prices

Transparent, fair and flexible

One lesson unit lasts 40 minutes.

Individual Lessons (40-minute units)

Single unit €33 / unit
Booking 6 consecutive weeks €30 / unit
Booking 12 consecutive weeks €25 / unit
The reduced rates for 6 or 12 consecutive weeks require either full prepayment of the entire package or a written instalment-payment agreement. In both cases, the full agreed amount for the booked package remains payable even if the participant discontinues the lessons early.

Intensive Training (2 units per week)

For anyone who wants to progress faster.

Flexible €55 / week
Booking 6 consecutive weeks €50 / week
Booking 12 consecutive weeks €42 / week
As with individual lessons, the reduced weekly rates for 6 or 12 weeks require either full prepayment of the entire package or a written instalment-payment agreement, with the full agreed amount remaining payable even if the participant discontinues the lessons early.

👥 Group Lessons

Online, for groups of three or more participants

Group unit (from 3 people) €15–20 / person
Group course from 10 units €12–17 / person
Group course over 20 units €10–15 / person

The stated price per person applies for a sufficiently large group; for a smaller group (minimum three people) the price per unit is calculated slightly higher accordingly. Larger groups benefit from an even lower price per person.

The discounted rates from 10 units, or from more than 20 units, likewise require either full prepayment of the entire course package or a written instalment-payment agreement. The full agreed amount remains payable in both cases even if individual participants leave the course early.

💛 Social Rate
